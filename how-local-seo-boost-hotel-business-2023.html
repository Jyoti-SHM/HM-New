How we Use the Best Local Seo Strategy in 2023 For Hotels & Resorts

In today's digital era having an account for your hotel or resort on Google My Business is a crucial step in boosting your business growth. This powerful platform enables customers to contact you easily by just clicking through the call button also and provides them with essential information, such as directions to your property and a website button which directly redirects to the website. Google My Business is extensively used by local stores and other brands to list their properties, making it a major hub for nearby locations and optimising the local Seo.

Here we are excited to share valuable insights that will empower your hotel business to reach new heights through the power of Local SEO for hotels and resorts. As the leading hotel management company Hospitality Minds we understand the importance of having an account of hotels and resorts in google my business. It continues to evolve, embracing effective digital marketing strategies is crucial, and local SEO is undoubtedly at the forefront.

We have been proudly serving hotels and resorts for over 7+ years, concentrating in digital marketing for hotels and resorts and revenue management. It allows us to share some invaluable tips on how we employ local Seo strategies to significantly enhance hotel occupancy, leading to an important increase in bookings and to get the valuable brand for our clients

Here are some tips which we used for hotels and resort local SEO :

Understanding the Impact of Hotel Local SEO :

As travellers increasingly rely on search engines and mobile devices to plan their trips, establishing a robust online presence has become non-negotiable. Local SEO, centred around location-specific keywords, enables your establishment to be easily discovered by potential guests seeking accommodations in your area. By optimising your Google My Business listing, your hotel or resort can secure a prominent position in search engine results, enhancing visibility and attracting more guests.

Crafting Your Local SEO Strategy :

To make your hotel stand out in local search results, it's essential to develop a tailored local SEO strategy. Begin by identifying the keywords that resonate with your location and target audience. Incorporate phrases like best hotel in —- location “hotel in location” to ensure your website aligns with what travellers are searching for and get the perfect result of our property.

Optimising Google My Business (GMB) Listing :

In today's digital landscape, your Google My Business listing is a powerful asset. By optimising it with accurate and up-to-date information, such as your hotel's name, address, and phone number, you signal to search engines that your business is relevant to local searches. Encourage guests to leave positive reviews, as they play a significant role in boosting your hotel's credibility.

Providing an Exceptional Mobile Experience :

Mobile-friendliness is a must! A seamless mobile experience delights guests and pleases search engines alike. Ensure your website loads quickly and adjusts to different screen sizes. This enhances user satisfaction and positively impacts your search engine rankings.

Empowering Your Advertising Efforts :

Geo Targeted advertising is a potent tool to attract local customers. By integrating keywords like "local SEO for hotels" and "Hotel local SEO" into your ad campaigns, you can effectively reach travellers searching for accommodations in your vicinity. This personalised approach maximises your chances of converting potential guests into actual bookings.

Managing the Google my reviews :

The most important part of local seo is to boost the reviews by using the keywords and also to reply to those reviews. Encourage your guests to leave reviews on your Google My Business listing. Positive reviews not only influence potential guests' decision-making but also play a crucial role in local search rankings. Responding to reviews, both positive and negative, also shows that you are engaged with your guests and care about their feedback, which can positively impact your local SEO efforts.

Conclusion

We Hospitality Minds place great emphasis on optimising Google My Business accounts to achieve the highest possible occupancy rates. By employing a comprehensive local SEO strategy, we consistently secure top positions in search results. Our primary goal is to ensure our clients' Google My Business accounts rank within the top 3 for high search volume keywords. This strategic approach not only generates more calls but also drives increased traffic to our clients' websites, resulting in a substantial boost in bookings.
