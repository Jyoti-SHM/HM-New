CASE STUDY: From Hidden to High-Demanded Resort.

Aayush Resort Karjat

Background

Aayush Resort a luxury 4 star Resort in Karjat—ideal for leisure stays, destination weddings, and short escapes. The product was strong, the experience was premium, but digitally, the resort was almost invisible.

There was no meaningful social media presence, no organic rankings, and minimal discovery across search platforms platforms. Despite being one of the best-positioned resorts in the region, it wasn’t part of the customer’s consideration set simply because people didn’t know it existed.

This created a clear disconnect: a high-potential resort with low digital awareness, resulting in missed bookings across leisure and wedding segments.

Objective

The focus was to build visibility, engagement, and demand across channels—turning Aayush Resort into a discoverable and desirable brand.

Key priorities included:

  • Growing reach and followers on social media
  • Driving meaningful engagement and content interaction
  • Increasing traffic and enquiries from digital platforms
  • Strengthening local and AI-driven search visibility
  • Improving organic calls and direct bookings
  • OTA - Revenue Management visibility, More business with better ARR.

Services Deployed

Website Redesigning SEO Social Media Marketing Revenue Management Performance Marketing

Challenges

The resort was starting from a weak digital foundation. Growth had stalled due to low reach and poor discoverability. Social media lacked consistency, storytelling, and visual appeal—making it difficult to engage or retain audience attention.

Occupancy was very low. Rooms were heavily dependent on weekend travelers only. Online visibility and pricing were not fully optimized for corporate or transit guests.

At the same time, the absence of organic traffic and search visibility meant the resort was missing out on high-intent customers actively searching for properties in Karjat.

👉 The core issue wasn’t the product—it was lack of visibility, consistency, and digital positioning.

Strategy & Execution

Hospitality Minds implemented a multi-channel growth strategy focused on building both awareness and conversion with 360 degree marketing and aggressive push. Our team did round visits at the property we understood its potential and executed one our key marketing approach which skyrocketed the presence and sales.

The website was redesigned to reflect the premium positioning of the resort while improving user experience and conversion flow. SEO efforts were aligned with high-intent keywords such as “resorts in Karjat,” “destination wedding venues,” and getaway-focused searches to build organic visibility.

On social media, the focus shifted to strong visual storytellingstorytelling—highlighting experiences, weddings, and lifestyle moments. Content was structured to drive both engagement and discovery, supported by performance marketing to amplify reach and attract the right audience.

On Revenue management, We introduced dynamic pricing to balance weekend demand and attract weekday bookings. Since the hotel has 115 rooms and it was getting difficult to manage rooms, we introduced a well Built and integrated PMS and Channel Manager which solved their issues and now the hotel operations are running smoothly. Designed special weekday offers for corporate guests, business travelers, and small groups. Improved online presence with better photos, descriptions, and targeted content highlighting weekday advantages. Actively managed guest reviews to strengthen the hotel’s online reputation. Worked with the hotel team to shift focus from just weekends to a balanced 7-day business strategy.

Simultaneously, Google Business Profile was optimized to improve local visibility and capture direct enquiries, while content was structured to improve presence in AI-driven search environments.

Results

The transformation was significant and directly impacted visibility and bookings:

  • 212.8% increase in calls through Google Business Profile
  • Noticeable growth in direct bookings from GBP
  • 70% improvement in organic rankings for key search terms
  • 1000+ new followers built through targeted social growth
  • Over 1 million views across 3 Instagram reels
  • 1 lakh+ monthly reach on social platforms
  • Strong improvement in destination wedding enquiries and bookings.
  • Bounce ratio improved by 61.22%
  • Average engagement time improved by 86.21%
  • Weekend strength continued with consistently high occupancy.
  • Weekday occupancy improved significantly, reducing dependency on weekends.
  • Average room rate and revenue grewbecause of a better mix of guests (leisure + corporate + transit).
  • Online rankings improved, bringing in more bookings across the week.
  • Guests rated the hotel better, leading to more repeat customers.
  • The hotel no longer relied only on weekend peaks.
  • Weekday business stabilized revenue, making cash flow more predictable.
  • More consistent occupancy throughout the week helped the property grow faster and stronger.

👉 The resort moved from being unknown to in-demand, especially in high-value segments like weddings and group bookings.

Conclusion

Aayush Resort didn’t need reinvention—it needed visibility.

By aligning SEO, social media, Revenue Managment and performance marketing, Hospitality Minds transformed it into a high-discovery, high-engagement brand that consistently attracts and converts demand.
